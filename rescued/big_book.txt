"THE COMPLETE AND UNABRIDGED HISTORY OF THE LOST PENGUIN"
==========================================================

Chapter 1 - The Egg
Once upon a time, in a server room very far from Antarctica, a
small egg was found on top of a rack, between a switch and a very
warm power supply. Nobody knew where it came from. The heat of the
servers kept it warm, and one morning, with a tiny CRACK, out came
a penguin. The coders called him Tux.

Chapter 2 - The Apprentice
Tux grew up watching the coders work. He noticed that the good
ones never touched the mouse. They typed. They pressed TAB. They
knew where they were thanks to pwd. They walked around the file
system with cd like it was their own igloo.

Chapter 3 - The Promise
One day, the oldest coder called Tux and whispered a secret
into his tiny ear. "Tux," she said, "if one day you must hide the
vault password, hide it in pieces. Only someone who really knows
the command line will be able to put it back together."

Chapter 4 - The Secret
And so Tux did. This is the piece he hid in this book:
